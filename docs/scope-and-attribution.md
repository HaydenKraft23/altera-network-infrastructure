# Scope and attribution

## Authorship and context

Altera is an academic team lab. Hayden Kraft led the infrastructure integration and
operational work represented in this portfolio. Other students and faculty contributed
to the broader project. This repository does not claim sole authorship of the entire lab.

The artifacts are reviewed through the checks described in the repository. The offline
tools here are refined, sanitized versions of exercises used in the lab; they are not
byte-for-byte exports of every original operational script.

## Simulated business scenario

The company and relay telemetry are fictional. Millisecond latencies in the fixture
model a training network, not physical interplanetary propagation. The satellite
transport design is a future virtual-router exercise, not deployed orbital hardware.

## Third-party software and images

Proxmox, Cisco software, Microsoft Windows Server, Ubuntu, NGINX, Wazuh, Prometheus,
Grafana, Loki, Forgejo, Nextcloud and Vaultwarden retain their own ownership and licenses.
No vendor VM images, binaries, screenshots, logos or third-party source distributions
are included. Obtain and license software through its authorized distributor.

The original team photos, humorous biographies and game-themed research documents
are excluded because they are not needed to demonstrate the engineering work.

## Publication and licensing

No repository-wide software license has been selected. Do not infer a license grant
for third-party software or team-owned work. Confirm contributor permissions before
adding a license or redistributing additional artifacts.

The examples use `example.test` and documentation-only IP ranges; replace them only
in your own authorized environment. This repository contains no working lab access details.
